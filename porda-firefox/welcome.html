2-minute walkthrough

⚡ Two quick things

  1. 1

    Pin PordaAI to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle icon in Chrome’s toolbar and pin PordaAI, so the on/off switch and cover settings are always one click away.

  2. 2

    Reload any tabs you already had open

    Press Ctrl + R on them once so PordaAI can start protecting them. Tabs you open from now on work right away.

v2.3.8 · Latest
  • A brand-new popup. Everything has been redesigned from scratch — cleaner typography, smoother switches and sliders, and a compact status strip across the top that tells you at a glance whether you are protected, without hunting through tabs.
  • Protection beyond the browser. If you have the PordaAI desktop app installed, the new Connect button in the popup links the two together in one click. Once connected it reads PC Sync · Connected, and detection runs on the desktop instead of in your browser — around 99% less browser load and up to 10× faster, covering every window on your screen, not just web pages.
  • The popup now speaks 11 languages. Pick yours from the language selector in the top-right corner. Arabic, Bengali, English, Persian, French, Hindi, Indonesian, Malay, Russian, Turkish and Urdu.
  • Image and video covers, side by side. Cover settings are split into two tabs so images and videos can be styled independently. Both can also be set to Greyscale, or to cover the whole image rather than just the detected area.
  • Easier to support the project. PordaAI is free and always will be. If you would like to help keep it that way, the support section now has a bKash number you can copy with a single click.
